# City Swirl Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-18
App: City Swirl (package com.gccompany.cityswirl, Android)
Contact: gccompany.dev@gmail.com

City Swirl ("the app") has no accounts of its own, purchases or developer-run server. Your game progress is stored in a file on your device and, while you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your Google Play Games "Saved Games" (cloud save). It is never sent to a server run by the developer. The information below is processed through the Google services the app uses.

## 1. Information collected and why

| Information | Details | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| App usage events (Firebase Analytics) | App open/close, session length, tutorial progress, round start/end and result, shop and ad button use | Balance the game, find where players drop off |
| App instance ID | A random identifier created per installation (not linked to your name or email) | Group events per installation |
| Device and app info | Device model, Android version, app version, language/country, graphics performance (frame rate) figures | Find device-specific performance problems |
| Crash information (Firebase Crashlytics) | Error records when the app crashes (including native crashes), device and app state | Fix bugs |
| Google Play Games sign-in | The app asks once whether to continue with Play Games or as a guest; with Play Games it signs in with the Play Games account already on the device (guests are not signed in and can switch later in the options). Your Play Games player ID is written in the save file on the device next to a random guest ID made by the app | Tie the cloud save to that player |
| Cloud save (Google Play Games Saved Games) | Your game progress (the save file's text: stars, rank, coins, collection, achievements, settings; not the per-device graphics measurements) and a one-line summary (stars, rank) | Restore your progress on a new device or after reinstalling |
| Leaderboard (Google Play Games leaderboards) | When signed in to Play Games: your total best score with a title and game-language tag, shown to other players with your Play Games nickname and avatar | Show rankings |
| Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config) | The app downloads game settings (e.g. ad limits); the app instance ID and similar data may be sent to Google | Tune the game |
| Advertising info (Google AdMob) - from the release version | Advertising ID (you can reset or delete it), IP address, ad impressions/clicks/views, device info | Show rewarded ads and measure ad revenue |

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or audio. The app uses the Internet, network state, vibration and notification permissions.

## 2. Third parties

The information above is sent to and processed by the following providers. We do not sell it and do not try to identify individuals with it.
- Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Google Play Games services (sign-in, Saved Games): https://policies.google.com/privacy ; Saved Games are kept in your Google account's storage.
- Google AdMob / Google advertising: https://policies.google.com/privacy , https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
- In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions requiring consent, a consent dialog may appear before ads; if you decline, only non-personalized ads may be shown. (To be finalized when ads are integrated.)

## 3. Retention and deletion

- The save file is on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Android's automatic backup is not used, and the save file is not included in a device-to-device transfer to a new phone either (on a new device it comes back from the Play Games cloud save).
- While you are signed in to Play Games, a copy is kept in Play Games Saved Games: it stays after uninstalling and comes back when you reinstall. "Erase all data" in the options deletes this cloud save together with the device save. You can also delete the game's data from the Play Games app (or your Google account settings).
- Data processed by Firebase and AdMob is retained according to those services' policies.
- Advertising ID: reset or delete it, and opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
- To request deletion of data tied to your app instance ID, email gccompany.dev@gmail.com. Reinstalling creates a new ID. The app currently has no in-app switch to turn off analytics.

## 4. Notifications

Only if you allow it, the app schedules local notifications on your device 1, 3 and 7 days after you leave the app. They are not server push messages and can be turned off in the options.

## 5. Sending logs by email

The app on the store has no feature for sending logs by email. Crash records are handled only by Firebase Crashlytics (section 1), and the app writes no log files outside its own storage, such as the device's Downloads folder.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact gccompany.dev@gmail.com and we will delete it.

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
